How to Use

Calculate Age mode:

  • Enter a From date (typically a date of birth)
  • Adjust the Target date if you need an age on a day other than today
  • The result updates automatically and shows the exact age in years, months, and days

Calculate Date mode:

  • Enter a date
  • Choose whether that date is the From Date (result = date + age) or the Target Date (result = date − age)
  • Enter an age in years, months, and days and the matching date is calculated instantly

Add to History & Share: Results compute automatically — use Add to History when you want to keep one for reference. The Share button copies a link that re-opens the calculator with the same inputs.

Age at end of year (31 Dec)

Underneath each Calculate Age result is a second line showing the age the person will be on 31 December of the target year. Most national and international athletics federations assign athletes to age groups (U15, U17, U18, U20, etc.) based on their age on the 31 December of the competition year, so this value is often more relevant than the literal age on competition day.

Calculate Date mode

Calculate Date mode answers questions like "what date is 25 years, 3 months, and 10 days after 5 April 2034?" or "if I'm 32 years and 4 months old today, when was I born?" — useful for working out the cut-off dates for an age-group competition.
