About

Athletics Utilities

Handy calculators for track and field athletes, coaches, and fans. Every tool is free, requires no signup, works in your browser, and can be installed as a Progressive Web App for offline use.

Start with the Pace & Speed Calculator to plan training sessions and race splits, use the World Athletics Score Calculator to convert any track or field performance into World Athletics (formerly IAAF) points, or work out a multi-event total with the Combined Event Score Calculator for decathlon, heptathlon, and pentathlon.
