NOTICE

Privacy

No tracking, no analytics, no cookies. Everything you type into a calculator stays on your device.

Privacy details

Short version: Athletics Utilities does not track you. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking scripts, and no account or signup. Anything you type into a calculator stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

The calculators use your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage to remember your preferences and recent calculations. This data is created and read only by your browser on this site — it is never sent to the maintainer of this website or to any third party.

  • Preferences (sessionStorage, cleared when you close the tab): the gender selection, the calculator mode and sub-mode toggles on the Pace, Time, and Age calculators, and the open/closed state of collapsible sections.
  • Theme preference (localStorage, persists until cleared): your choice of Dark or Light theme.
  • Calculation history (localStorage, persists until you clear it): the most recent ten calculations on each of the Pace, Time, and Age calculators.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing site data for athleticsutils.com in your browser settings, or by using the in-app history controls on each calculator.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies.

Analytics and tracking

There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no fingerprinting, no error-reporting service, and no third-party script of any kind.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by a third-party hosting provider, which may record standard request metadata for security and reliability under its own privacy policy. The site maintainer does not have access to those logs.

Donations

The donation link on the home page goes to PayPal. If you choose to donate, PayPal handles the payment under its own privacy policy.
